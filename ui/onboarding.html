One key. Any app.

Put your cursor in any text field, then:

1 · Tap Left Ctrl

Koovu starts listening. You'll hear a pop and see a small bubble near your cursor.

2 · Talk naturally

English, Tamil, Tanglish — mid-sentence corrections are fine, Koovu resolves them.

3 · Tap Left Ctrl again

Your words land as polished text, right where your cursor is.

You can change the hotkey later in Settings.

Two permissions

macOS needs your OK for Koovu to hear you and type for you.

Microphone

Koovu may not appear in the list until you allow the popup. In Settings → Permissions, tap Request access, click Allow, then Koovu shows up here.

Accessibility

To type into any app and catch your hotkey. Koovu usually appears here right away — toggle it on.

Accessibility and Microphone are separate lists in macOS — seeing one does not mean the other is done.
