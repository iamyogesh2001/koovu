Privacy

No middleman. Ever.

Wispr Flow routes your voice through their servers. Koovu has no servers — the app runs on your Mac and talks straight to the AI provider with your keys.

Your keys, your accounts

You bring free API keys from Groq and Deepgram. Your audio goes directly to them under your own account — we never see or store your voice.

audio → provider. koovu ∉ path.

Everything lives on your machine

Dictionary, corrections, history, stats — all in a local folder you own. Delete it and Koovu forgets you existed.

~/.koovu/config.json

Open source, fully offline soon

Read every line on GitHub. Local Whisper and Ollama support are on the roadmap — a mode where nothing ever leaves your Mac.
